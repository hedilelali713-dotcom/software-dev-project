Partner 3 - Future Jobs and Conclusion



So, will AI actually replace software developers in the future?



From what I researched, I think AI will change programming jobs, but it probably won't replace developers completely.



According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, jobs for software developers, quality assurance analysts, and testers are expected to grow by 15% between 2024 and 2034.



This is interesting because it shows that there's still expected to be demand for people working in software development.



Developers also do more than just write code. They solve problems, design software, understand what people need, and check that everything works properly.



So our group's conclusion is that AI will probably become a tool that developers use more often, rather than completely replacing them.



The important thing for future developers is learning how to use AI while still building their own programming skills.
