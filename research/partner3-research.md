What I researched



For my part, I'm looking into whether AI could replace software developers in the future.



AI is getting better at writing code and helping with programming tasks, so I think it could change the way developers work.

Research finding



According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, employment for software developers, quality assurance analysts, and testers is projected to grow by 15% from 2024 to 2034.



I think this is interesting because even though AI is becoming more common, there is still expected to be demand for people working in software development.



This doesn't mean AI won't change programming jobs, but it suggests that developers will still be needed in the future.
